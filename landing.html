Questions

What do I need to take a good photo?

Hold the phone square to the page, get close enough to read the small print, and avoid shadows.

How are floors worked out?

Floors are 20 ft apart starting at 100 ft, so Level 1 is 100 to 120 ft. North and East columns are every 100 ft.

Is it always right?

No. It is a helper, not a replacement for the drawing. Check what it tells you.

Does it cost anything?

The public version has a daily limit on reads. Ask about an access code.
