Tips for a clear photo
  • Lay the iso flat and shoot straight down, so the page edges are square in the photo.
  • Fill the frame with the pipe drawing. Tiny numbers need pixels, so get close enough that the drawing covers most of the photo.
  • Use bright, even light. Keep your shadow and glare off the page.
  • Take a real photo, or use Scan Documents and pick the saved scan. A screenshot of a camera preview is too small.
  • Tap the drawing on your screen to focus before you shoot.

How it works

  1. Photo. Load a clear photo of the whole sheet and tap Read the iso for me. Claude reads the coordinates printed at the pipe ends, then the material lists and the circled numbers and tags on the drawing.
  2. Find. You get the floor and the North and East columns in plain words, with the spots on the drawing marked. Type the N and E of the column you are standing by and it tells you which way to walk. Trace the full route only if you want to narrow it down by floor or column.
  3. Check. Walk the line and tick off the pipe, elbows, tees, valves, flanges, gaskets, bolts, tags like J1 and J2, supports, welds and cut lengths. Each item has a plain-English name. Your ticks are saved on this phone.

Zoom in before you tap for better aim. Red messages mean a length or coordinate doesn't add up.
